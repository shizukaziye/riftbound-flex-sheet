Riftbound Flex Sheet

Every legend with at least two placings across the three big cuts, or a top 4 at any 100+ player event in the last month. The flex sheet shows every card seen and the most copies any list ran. The generic list builds one consensus 40 / 10 / 3 list per legend and marks the slots where lists disagree.

Reading the sheet. The number is the max copies seen (a 1-of in one list, 0 in another, 2 in a third → shows as 2). The bar is how many of that legend's lists ran the card at all. Rows tagged core were in every list. Separate tallies main deck and sideboard on their own; Combined adds each list's main and sideboard copies first, then takes the max, which is the count you need to own to build any one list.

Reading the generic list. Main deck and sideboard are treated as one 50-card pool. Every copy of every card is a slot, scored by the share of lists that ran at least that many copies across main + sideboard (over lists that posted a sideboard). The 50 best slots make the pool, then the 40 that most often sat in the main deck become the main deck and the other 10 the sideboard. The three cells on each row are the pool shares for the 1st, 2nd and 3rd copy: filled = locked (two thirds or more), outlined = flex, dotted = a copy this list keeps in the sideboard. Hover a cell for the main / side split.

Adjusting the list. On any row, − drops a copy, + adds one (up to 3 in total), and ▾S / ▴M move one between main and sideboard. Anything you drop below the generic count shows under Removed with add-back buttons; Next in line and the add box bring in cards that were never in. Counts turn red when the list is off 40 / 10 / 3. Edits are kept per legend in this browser until you press Reset.

Click any row for the copy split: how many lists ran it at 3, 2 or 1 copies or not at all, their share, and the average placing of each group. Average placing mixes big cuts (1–64) with smaller events where every list is a top 4, so read it as a hint, not a ranking.
