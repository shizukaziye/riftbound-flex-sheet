How to read this page

Sample. Every event with 100+ players in the last month, cut to a depth that scales with the field: 100+ top 4 · 200+ top 8 · 400+ top 16 · 800+ top 32 · 1600+ top 64. A legend is listed with at least two placings inside those cuts.

Reading the sheet. The number is the max copies seen (a 1-of in one list, 0 in another, 2 in a third → shows as 2). The bar is how many of that legend's lists ran the card at all. Rows tagged core were in every list. Separate tallies main deck and sideboard on their own; Combined adds each list's main and sideboard copies first, then takes the max, which is the count you need to own to build any one list.

Reading the generic list. Main deck and sideboard are treated as one 50-card pool. Every copy of every card is a slot, scored by the share of lists that ran at least that many copies across main + sideboard (over lists that posted a sideboard). The 50 best slots make the pool, then the 40 that most often sat in the main deck become the main deck and the other 10 the sideboard. The three cells on each row are the pool shares for the 1st, 2nd and 3rd copy: filled = locked (two thirds or more), outlined = flex, dotted = a copy this list keeps in the sideboard. Rows are sorted by energy cost within each type (the pill before the name; a gold number beside it is the power cost). Hover a cell for the main / side split. The last number on each row is the average placing of the lists that ran at least that many copies, green when a point or more better than the sample average and red when a point or more worse; the header carries the mean over every copy you have picked, next to the generic list's figure once you edit.

Adjusting the list. On any row, − drops a copy, + adds one (up to 3 in total), and ▾S / ▴M move one between main and sideboard. Anything you drop below the generic count shows under Removed with add-back buttons; Next in line and the add box bring in cards that were never in. Counts turn red when the list is off 40 / 10 / 3. Edits are kept per legend in this browser until you press Reset. Build it opens a checklist of the current list with set, card number, rarity and price per card, sorted by set and number so cards are easy to find in a binder or a store; tick what you own and it tracks what is left to buy. Save keeps a named copy under the legend (Load, Link, delete from its chip), Link copies a URL that carries the whole list so it opens on any machine, and Export / Import in the bar move every save between browsers as text.

Reading the buy list. Turn legends on and off with the tiles. For each legend that is on, the list uses whatever that legend's list is right now on this page: your edited or loaded copy if you have one, otherwise the generic list under the current event-size and placing filters, plus as many of its next-in-line flex slots as you choose. Legend cards and the 12 runes are included. One at a time needs the most copies any single deck uses; All at once sums every deck. Tick cards as you buy them.

Reading the cards table. Every card in the game, with the share of sampled lists that ran it anywhere (main, sideboard, battlefields, runes, or as the legend) under the current event-size and placing filters. The share is out of the lists that could play it: lists whose legend has every domain of the card, or every list for colorless cards. Where shows which legends ran it and the share of each legend's lists that did. Filter by rarity, set, type and domain, or search; "Not played" shows what never appeared. Sets and rarities come from each card's base printing on TCGplayer.

Prices are the TCGplayer market price of the cheapest printing of each card (base, alternate art, promo or overnumbered, whichever is lowest); hover a price for the printing and the lowest single listing, click it to open TCGplayer. Prices over $20 are red. The generic-list header totals the whole list including the legend card and 12 runes.

Click any row for the copy split: how many lists ran it at 3, 2 or 1 copies or not at all, their share, and the average placing of each group. Average placing mixes deep cuts (down to 64th) with shallow ones (top 4), so a card common at small events reads better than it is; use the event-size filter to compare like with like.

Sources: every tournament on the riftdecks.com tournament index with 100 or more players dated 9 Aug to 9 Sep 2026, pulled 9–10 Sep 2026. The counted cut scales with the field: 100+ players top 4, 200+ top 8, 400+ top 16, 800+ top 32, 1600+ top 64. A legend is listed when it has at least two placings inside those cuts. Each list link is event and place; hover for date, field size and the cut counted. RQ = Regional Qualifier, RO = Regional Open, CC = S4 City Challenge. Card types and domains from the riftdecks card database. Prices: TCGplayer market price of the cheapest printing per card, via the daily scrape behind tcg-price-tracker, as of the date shown in the header.
